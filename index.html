How this is calculated. Position size = funds × margin deployed × leverage. Each round trip charges two orders, at the bps rate on notional. Commission is your share of gross fees — calculated on list price, before the discount your traders receive. The discount is borne by Niyam and does not reduce your payout.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Actual earnings depend entirely on how your traders behave — how often they trade, at what size, and for how long. Nothing here is a projection of returns for traders, a promise of income for you, or investment advice. Fees shown exclude GST. Commission payouts are subject to applicable taxes, withholding and the partner agreement.
